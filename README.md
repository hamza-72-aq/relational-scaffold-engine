# Relational Scaffold Engine (RSE) v1.0.0
### An Open-Source Algorithmic Protocol for Automating Social Capital Planning and Mitigating Institutional Voids in Frontier Economies

---

## 1. Defining the Core Engine: What is the SCPM?
The **Social Capital Planning Conceptual Model (SCPM)** is an endogenous, practice-led community development framework designed to assist in planning, measuring, and tracking the relational networks required to mobilize local resources. Developed over 11 years of hands-on subnational field execution, the SCPM rejects orthodox development models that treat social capital as a passive, post-hoc evaluative byproduct. 

Instead, the model operationalizes human relationships as primary architectural inputs. It disaggregates abstract macroeconomic goals into granular, procedural tasks carried out across distinct bonding, bridging, and linking ties. By treating social capital as a "sink plug" that prevents introduced resources from draining away due to systemic corruption, the SCPM establishes structural community capacity prior to any external capital market intervention.

## 2. Technical Architecture & LFA Integration Logic
This repository automates the SCPM into machine-readable graph logic. Traditional project management tools—specifically the linear Logical Framework Approach (LFA)—fail in high-void settings because they assume a mature legal and institutional canopy exists as a baseline input. 

The Relational Scaffold Engine (RSE) replaces this linear setup with a **Dynamic Closed-Loop Diagnostic System**:
*   **The Inversion Trigger (Conditional Deferral Safeguard):** The `scpm_engine.py` script parses ground-level field logs (interaction frequency, peer mentorship metrics, and narrative consensus) to calculate a normalized *Programmatic Trust Audit Density*. If local trust falls below the critical **75% Particularized Trust threshold**, the engine flags high interface friction, automatically freezes funding allocation lines inside the project Logframe, and diverts the project into a programmed *Trust-Building Gestation* cycle.
*   **Automated Task Inversion Tracking:** The protocol maps the community's specialized teams as distributed task nodes. As the collective's internal capacity matures, the engine dynamically transitions operational weight to the community collective, scaling down external administrative permissions to guarantee a data-verified, sustainable exit.
